Recovery controls

prepare accepts identity key registration / skeleton / name / fold, and original-name policy preserve / nfc. Keep the plan scoped to both independently verified owners.

preview accepts a limit in utf16 / scalar / grapheme units, a count (1–64), bidi policy raw / isolate / isolate-first, and formatting policy raw / escape / reject.
The review line must preserve at least four complete clusters per sample and fit its encoded byte budget. Test both requirements. Its dynamic field and fixed labels must retain separate visual positions. Keep suspicious formatting visible to the reviewer.

verify checks the current mapping, owner evidence, preview and rollback readiness without applying changes. Changing the plan or preview invalidates the previous dry run. commit applies the verified split once.
