Replay controls

Compare titles with collate: en-US or sv-SE, primary / secondary / tertiary.
Primary comparison can group accents or case. Regional alphabets can keep distinctions that English groups.

Replay inputs: locale, strength, cursor strategy, page size (1–64), and start cursor.
  title      advance past the last title weight
  composite  advance past the last title weight and immutable record ID
  inclusive  resume at the last record (legacy boundary compatibility)
Use start to replay from the beginning. A copied or captured cursor resumes after a boundary. It carries its snapshot, collation profile, locale, strength, strategy and page size.

Support needs a complete, duplicate-free replay with an unambiguous boundary before it can recover the real appeals.
