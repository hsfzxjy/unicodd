prepare stages an identity mapping for the two independently verified owners.
  Identity key     registration / skeleton / name / fold
  Original names   preserve / nfc

preview renders an audit line for each sample.
  Limit units      utf16 / scalar / grapheme
  Field limit      1–64
  Bidi policy      raw / isolate / isolate-first
  Controls         raw / escape / reject

Keep at least four complete clusters per sample. The UTF-8 budget covers the whole line. Keep the dynamic field and fixed labels in separate visual positions and make suspicious formatting visible.

verify checks the current mapping, owner evidence, preview and rollback readiness without applying changes. Changing the plan or preview invalidates the previous dry run. commit applies the verified split once.
