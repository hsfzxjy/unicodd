Receiver protocol spool/2
Each frame starts with a two-byte unsigned length, most significant byte first.
Length counts payload octets; the prefix itself is excluded. Maximum payload is 256 bytes.
A zero length is a heartbeat. Incomplete prefix or payload at EOF and oversized lengths are transport errors.

deframe FRAMING DECODER ERRORS INTEGRITY CHUNKS
FRAMING: be, le, scalars. DECODER: strict, chunks, replacement.
ERRORS: stop, quarantine. INTEGRITY: raw, nfc.
CHUNKS: network, whole, or comma-separated interior byte offsets.

The older modes are retained for local comparison. A trusted length establishes the next frame boundary even if the current payload fails UTF-8 validation. The transport offsets are independent of the wire lengths.
